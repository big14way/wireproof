# The 3-minute video

Built with the pitch-video skill: `demo.json` is the cut (scene order, narration, clip cuts), `scenes.json`, `scenes2.json` and `scenes3.json` record the app screens with Playwright against `localhost:3000`, and `make-vo.sh` synthesises the narration (Edge neural voice) where the speech service is reachable. The MP4, clips and build cache live outside the repo in `~/Developer/wireproof-video/`.

The phone scenes (start a job, talk to it) are a screen recording a working electrician made on their own phone using the live site. The board and cable footage is our own.

Footage from other creators, credited here rather than on screen: eFIXX (flame test on fake cable), Snag Inspector (undersized cable) and E'LIGHT ELECTRICALS LTD (conduit in a block wall), all TikTok; the plastering and Lagos aerial clips are from Pexels. `SCRIPT.md` is the narration.
