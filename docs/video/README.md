# The 3-minute video

Built with the pitch-video skill: `demo.json` is the cut (scene order, narration, clip cuts), `scenes.json` and `scenes2.json` record the app screens with Playwright against `localhost:3000`, and `make-vo.sh` synthesises the narration (Edge neural voice) where the speech service is reachable. The MP4, clips and build cache live outside the repo in `~/Developer/wireproof-video/`.

Footage credited on screen: Pa Chi Tech, E'LIGHT ELECTRICALS LTD and Bright Sky Electrical (TikTok); the plastering and Lagos aerial clips are from Pexels. The board and cable footage is our own. `SCRIPT.md` is the narration.
